# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in SimpleLauncher welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban participants whose behavior is harmful to the project community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with care.
